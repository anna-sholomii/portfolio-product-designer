About

Hi - I'm Anna.
Product designer

Based in Toronto 3+ years of experience
01How I started

My design journey
started with learning fast.

I found out about UX design while doing my Master's in Finance, and it completely hijacked my attention. A few months of intense courses and training later, I landed my first job at an early-stage startup.

Small startup meant no mentors to rely on, but I was learning fast through the continuous feedback from our users and the team: engineers, customer support, sales, founders.

02Project beside my work

Volunteering for a government
on an educational project.

Live today
~
2000
students

reading more and tracking their own growth - on a platform I designed from the ground up.

I designed a reading platform for a national Department of Education in Ukraine - working with a small engineering team (2 front-end, 2 back-end) to get students reading more and track how their skills grew.

It shipped, has shown continuous user growth, and is now being rolled out to the European market.

03Staying sharp

My favourite classroom
is a hackathon.

I keep showing up to hackathons and design workshops - partly for the skills, mostly for the people. Give me a room full of new faces and a ticking clock, and that's where my best ideas show up.

04Outside of work

When I'm not
designing.

You'll find me at the gym, deep in a book about cognitive biases (handy in design, hazardous at dinner parties), or attempting a recipe I've never tried.

05Principles of my work

Three things I keep
coming back to.

01

Talk to everyone

Engineers, sales, founders, users - the best calls rarely come from the design tool. They come from the conversation.

02

Ship fast, learn faster

Get something real in front of people early, collect feedback fast, and adjust - rather than polishing in isolation.

03

Make it last

The work I'm proudest of is still running years later. That's the standard I hold myself to.

That's the short version.
The long one I'd be happy to tell in person.
